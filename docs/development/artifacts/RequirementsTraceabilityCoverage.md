# Requirements traceability coverage

Generated by `script/investigation.sh` from `openspec/specs/**/spec.md` and the suites' own annotations. A requirement's
`Status` is presence-based: `Tested` means every declared scenario carries a committed system case or an API-suite entry
and CI is green. `Evidence` names what proves a scenario — `System suite`, `API suite`, `Proven in Rust`, or a
combination — so an empty cell is a scenario no committed suite claims. A `Type` is the kind the scenario names, the
kind its case declares once, the kind `test/api/scenarios.toml` gives it, or the kind its `// proves:` comment names
after an em dash; `Cause` and `N/A` cells are authored by hand and are never overwritten.
